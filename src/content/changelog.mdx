<Update date="2026-10-20" title="First version of the docs" tags={["docs", "circlekit"]}>
  A [Quickstart](/quickstart) that runs a paid endpoint with no wallet, a
  [guide](/guides/pay-from-an-agent) that makes a test payment on Arc Testnet,
  and an [API reference](/api/introduction) for the example endpoint.

  The pages are tested against circlekit commit `1a05260`, the merge of
  [vyperlang/circle-titanoboa-sdk#5](https://github.com/vyperlang/circle-titanoboa-sdk/pull/5).
  In tests, Gateway rejected 4- and 6-day payment authorizations and accepted
  7 days, and
  circlekit up to `09828f3` signed for 4 days by default. If you installed an
  older commit, reinstall with the command in the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
</Update>
